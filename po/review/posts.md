# Posts asking for a translation review

One short post per language, to paste into a language group or forum (Facebook groups, the Linux Mint forums' language sections). Each links to that language's review page.

The posts don't ask whether the translation is correct: a quick look answers "yes, it's fine", since it usually is. They ask which lines a native speaker would write differently, the way their language's software says it. That's the review that improves a translation (the Spanish one got it from axrusar on the Linux Mint forums, who reworded about half of it). Replies with a number and a better wording are all that's needed.
